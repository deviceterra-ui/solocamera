# Profession preset library — v0.51

The editor now includes 50 additional profession designs: five each for Technology, Agriculture, Medicine, Education, Business, Finance, Food & hospitality, Beauty & fitness, Construction, and Creative media. The complete built-in title catalog contains 138 designs.

## Add a design

1. Open an editing project.
2. Choose Text → Profession presets, or Creator tools → Profession presets.
3. Open Professions and use the profession selector to filter the designs.
4. Tap a design. Edit the headline, subtitle, label, font and colors while watching the preview.
5. Choose the duration and entrance/exit animation. Use the glow slider and particle switch to tune the finish.
6. Tap Preview animation + sound to hear the entrance accent once. Switch synchronized entrance sound off for a silent title, or lower its volume.
7. Tap Add to timeline. The preset appears as an editable text object on its own track.

Move and resize the object in the preview. Trim or move its timeline item independently. Its short entrance sound follows the object automatically and disappears when that object is deleted. Sound does not create an unrelated audio track. Select the title and open Text to change its sound, volume, particles or glow after insertion; Edit design text & colors opens the full designer.

Five layout families provide circuit/edge panels, halo panels, name reveals, glass expert plates, and full-frame presentation cards. Each profession has original vector pictograms and a coordinated two-color palette. Floating particles and the traveling sheen continue during the hold; the components reveal in sequence at the entrance and leave in sequence at the exit. Still mode disables animation and its entrance sound.

## Lightweight assets

All 50 pictograms are original vector drawings. Glow and particles use bounded drawing primitives, with cached title plates for the hold. No AI model or effect-video download is needed. Original mono WAV accents are synthesized once and cached locally (45,644 bytes per used preset, about 2.3 MB if all 50 are used). Preview and MP4/audio export use the same title-linked timing and volume.
