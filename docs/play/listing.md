# Store listing draft — Solo Camera

**Short description** (draft): Shoot solo with remote phone monitoring, a teleprompter, screen recording and editing.

**Full description** (draft):

Solo Camera helps creators capture and edit videos from their Android phones. Use one phone as the camera and a second as a monitor over the same Wi-Fi network or the camera phone's hotspot. Pair with a QR code, see the live preview and change supported camera settings remotely.

Read a script with the teleprompter, record your screen for tutorials, and add a movable presenter camera bubble or drawing annotations. In Studio, combine video, images, text and audio on a multitrack timeline, then export a finished video or audio file. The Windows receiver can send a phone-camera or screen-demo feed into OBS.

Camera settings and recording formats depend on each phone's hardware. Network preview latency varies with the device and connection. Schedule & Publish currently saves drafts on the phone; automatic posting to social platforms is not available yet. Optional AI tools may require a separate download where offered.

**Category recommendation:** Video Players & Editors. **Target audience:** creators, not children. **Monetization:** no paid features or advertising in the current source. The owner must confirm all choices in Play Console.

**Screenshots to capture from the release build:** home, camera controls, phone pairing/monitor preview, teleprompter, screen recording, Studio timeline, export, and local publishing drafts. Do not use prior debug screenshots as release proof without checking the build and disclosure text.
