Solo Camera privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Solo Camera, Android package studio.solocamera · Publisher: deviceterra-ui

Solo Camera uses the camera, microphone, screen capture and files you select to create videos. This policy describes the current Android release. The app does not require a Solo Camera account.

Information the app handles

When you choose to record, the app captures camera video, microphone or supported device audio, and optionally your screen. It stores recordings and exported edits on your phone. Projects, scripts, captions, editing choices, drafts and app settings are stored locally. You may import photos, video, audio and fonts through Android's file picker. You control what you import and export.

Network and sharing

Camera monitoring and live screen demos send preview video, audio where enabled, and control data over your local Wi-Fi or hotspot to a phone or Windows receiver that you pair and approve. People with access to that receiving device can view the content you transmit. Keep pairing codes private and use trusted networks. The app does not automatically upload your recordings to a Solo Camera server. Its Publish Studio currently saves local drafts; automatic social publishing is not active in this release.

If you choose to download an optional AI model or font, the app contacts the model or font host over HTTPS to fetch that file. The current model host is Google Cloud Storage and optional font downloads use GitHub's raw-content host. Those hosts may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address. The app has no app-operated advertising or analytics service in this release.

Permissions

Camera and microphone permissions support filming and the presenter camera. Screen recording requires Android's separate screen-capture consent. Internet and network-state access support approved local pairing and optional downloads. Display-over-other-apps access enables the movable presenter bubble and drawing tools when you turn them on. Notifications and foreground-service access keep capture controls visible while recording. You can revoke these permissions in Android Settings; the related feature may then stop working.

Retention, deletion and security

Recordings and exports stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete projects and drafts in the app and recordings or exports in your device's file or gallery app. Uninstalling the app removes its private settings, projects and downloaded models but may leave media saved in shared device storage. Locally paired connections use the app's approved pairing flow. Optional external downloads use HTTPS. No internet transmission is risk-free.

Children, changes and contact

Solo Camera is intended for creators and is not designed for children. We may update this page as features or data practices change; the effective date will be revised. For privacy questions or deletion help, contact madeinbrooklynbro+solocamera@gmail.com.
